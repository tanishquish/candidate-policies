LWV Greenwich Candidates Debate: Inflation, Affordability, Taxes, Reproductive Rights, Gun Safety
By: GREENWICHFREEPRESS | October 16, 2026
Candidates for State Senate 36th district Democrat Jill Oberlander and Republican Joe Kelly at the Oct 14, 2026 LWV Greenwich debate.
Mr. Kelly talked about removing the public benefit charge from electricity bills and urged a reduction in taxes to stimulate Connecticut’s economy to attract businesses.
Oberlander said, “What I won’t do is make promises that can’t be implemented – like cutting taxes or public benefit programs that shift programs from one pocket to another.”
Mr. Kelly said, “You have to cut the taxes. We’re on a slippery slope right now. We’re in a time of prosperity, but we’re suffering,” he said.
“I would work hard to bring money back to our district and I would not support new taxes,” she added.
Kelly and Oberlander were asked about reproductive rights and abortion.
On Wednesday, Mr. Kelly said his opinion was in favor of abortion rights.
“I’m pro-choice,” he said, adding, “I was brought up as a Catholic with 16 years of Catholic education. I have three strong daughters and a strong wife. There’s no way I can argue any other way.”
“These days it goes beyond saying, ‘I support the right to choose,'” she continued, adding it was also important to support doctors, healthcare providers and access to services including prescriptions and counseling.
Oberlander said she would have voted in favor of the legislation.
“And I will vote that way for any gun violence prevention bill. Anything that will keep our children and residents safe from gun violence, I will be a strong supporter of.”
Mr. Kelly, in stark contrast to Senator Fazio and State Rep Courpas, said he also would have voted in favor of the legislation.
“If a device is going to turn a handgun into an automatic weapon, that is bad. We should not vote in favor of that,” he said. I don’t want a device that will turn a handgun into an automatic weapon.”
